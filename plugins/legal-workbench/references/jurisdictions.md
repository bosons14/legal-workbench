# Jurisdiction quick reference

Orientation only - verify specifics. Always confirm the current position of any
statute, rule or court practice cited.

## India
**Courts:** Supreme Court -> High Courts (25) -> District Courts / Sessions Courts ->
Civil Judge / Judicial Magistrate. Tribunals: NCLT/NCLAT (company, insolvency),
NCDRC/State/District Consumer Commissions, ITAT, CESTAT, NGT, DRT/DRAT, SAT, CAT,
APTEL, TDSAT. Commercial Courts / Commercial Divisions of HCs for commercial disputes
above the specified value.

**Key procedural codes:** CPC 1908; Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023 (replaced
CrPC); Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (replaced IPC); Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023
(replaced Evidence Act) - all in force 1 July 2024 for new matters; older matters
may still be governed by the earlier codes. Limitation Act 1963. Commercial Courts
Act 2015 (pre-institution mediation, s.12A; strict written statement timelines).
Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. IBC 2016. DPDP Act 2023 (with rules).

**Citation forms:**
- Supreme Court: `(2017) 10 SCC 1`; `AIR 2017 SC 4161`; neutral citation
  `2023 INSC 123`; SCC OnLine: `2024 SCC OnLine SC 456`.
- High Courts: `2023 SCC OnLine Del 1234`; `AIR 2010 Bom 45`; neutral citations
  (e.g. `2023:DHC:1234`).
- Statutes: `Section 34, Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996`; `Order VII Rule 11, CPC`;
  `Article 226, Constitution of India`.
- Party names: `K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India`.

**Drafting conventions:** cause title with court, case type and number; "MOST
RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH"; numbered paragraphs; prayer clause; verification;
affidavit in support; list of dates and synopsis for SC/HC petitions; index and
court fee per the relevant state Court Fees Act. Advocates' legal notices are sent
on letterhead, often by Registered Post AD and email.

**Primary sources:** indiacode.nic.in, egazette.gov.in, main.sci.gov.in /
sci.gov.in, ecourts.gov.in, HC websites, judgments.ecourts.gov.in, Indian Kanoon
(free, useful for finding judgments - verify against the certified/official copy).

## United States
**Courts:** Federal - Supreme Court -> 13 Courts of Appeals -> 94 District Courts
(+ bankruptcy, Court of Federal Claims, Tax Court). Each state has its own
hierarchy (names vary - in New York the "Supreme Court" is the trial court).

**Rules:** FRCP, FRAP, FRE, local rules and individual judges' practices (always check).
State rules differ substantially.

**Citation (Bluebook):** `Brown v. Bd. of Educ., 347 U.S. 483, 495 (1954)`;
`Smith v. Jones, 123 F.4th 456, 460 (9th Cir. 2024)`; `42 U.S.C. § 1983 (2018)`;
`Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b)(6)`. Short forms: `Id. at 461`; `Smith, 123 F.4th at 461`.

**Primary sources:** supremecourt.gov, uscourts.gov, govinfo.gov, uscode.house.gov,
ecfr.gov, congress.gov, state legislature and court sites; CourtListener (free
opinions and RECAP dockets).

## England & Wales (UK)
**Courts:** UK Supreme Court -> Court of Appeal -> High Court (KBD, Chancery, Family;
Business and Property Courts) -> County Court / Crown Court / Magistrates.
Scotland and Northern Ireland have separate systems.

**Rules:** Civil Procedure Rules (CPR) and Practice Directions; pre-action protocols
(costs consequences for non-compliance).

**Citation (OSCOLA):** `Donoghue v Stevenson [1932] AC 562 (HL)`; neutral:
`R (Miller) v Secretary of State [2017] UKSC 5, [2018] AC 61 [12]`; statutes:
`Limitation Act 1980, s 5`. No full stops in abbreviations.

**Primary sources:** legislation.gov.uk, bailii.org, caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk,
supremecourt.uk, justice.gov.uk (CPR).

## Other common-law systems
Singapore (SGCA/SGHC, Rules of Court 2021, eLitigation), Australia (HCA, Federal
Court, state Supreme Courts; AGLC citation), Canada (SCC; McGill Guide), UAE onshore
(civil law) vs DIFC/ADGM courts (common law). Ask before assuming.
