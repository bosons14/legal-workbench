# Contract review checklist

Tick every heading; write "N/A - why" when a heading doesn't apply.

## Commercial core
- Parties: correct legal names, entity numbers (CIN/LLPIN, company no.), addresses, signatories' authority
- Recitals consistent with operative terms
- Scope / services / deliverables precisely defined; acceptance criteria and deemed acceptance
- Price, currency, taxes (GST/VAT/withholding - gross-up?), invoicing, payment period, late interest
  (India MSME: MSMED Act 2006 s.15-16 - 45-day maximum and compound interest for MSME suppliers [VERIFY])
- Price changes, indexation, most-favoured-customer
- Minimum commitments, exclusivity, volume

## Performance
- Service levels, service credits (sole remedy?), chronic failure termination right
- Timelines, time of essence, delay consequences, liquidated damages (genuine pre-estimate? cap?)
- Dependencies on the customer; relief for customer-caused delay
- Subcontracting, key personnel, change control

## Risk allocation
- Warranties: scope, duration, disclaimers (fitness/merchantability), remedies
- Indemnities: triggers (IP infringement, third-party claims, data breach, breach of law),
  procedure (notice, control of defence, settlement consent), caps/carve-outs
- Limitation of liability: cap amount and basis (fees paid / payable, period), exclusions
  of indirect/consequential loss and loss of profit, carve-outs (fraud, wilful misconduct,
  death/personal injury, IP indemnity, confidentiality, data protection) - is the cap mutual?
- Insurance requirements
- Force majeure: definition, notice, mitigation, termination after prolonged FM; payment obligations excluded?

## IP and data
- Ownership of deliverables, background vs foreground IP, licences (scope, term, territory, sublicensing)
- Open-source and third-party components; AI-generated output ownership and training-use restrictions
- Confidentiality: definition, exclusions, duration, permitted disclosures, return/destruction
- Data protection: roles (controller/processor; Data Fiduciary/Processor under DPDP Act 2023),
  DPA terms, security standards, breach notification timelines, sub-processors, cross-border transfer,
  audit rights, deletion at end
- Publicity / use of name and logo

## Term and exit
- Term, renewal (auto-renew notice windows), termination for convenience (notice, fees)
- Termination for cause: material breach, cure period, insolvency triggers (check IBC moratorium enforceability)
- Consequences: transition assistance, data return, survival clause, refunds of prepaid fees
- Non-compete / non-solicit (enforceability by jurisdiction)

## Boilerplate that bites
- Governing law; dispute resolution - courts vs arbitration (seat, venue, rules, institution,
  number of arbitrators, language); interim relief; escalation/mediation steps
  (India: pre-institution mediation for commercial suits, Commercial Courts Act s.12A [VERIFY])
- Assignment and change of control
- Entire agreement, order of precedence, amendments in writing, waiver, severability
- Notices: addresses, email validity, deemed receipt
- Counterparts and e-signatures (India: IT Act 2000 - some documents excluded, e.g. negotiable
  instruments other than cheques, powers of attorney, trusts, wills, contracts for sale of
  immovable property [VERIFY current First Schedule])
- Stamp duty and registration (India: state Stamp Acts; unstamped instruments and admissibility)
- Anti-bribery, sanctions, export control, modern slavery, ESG clauses
- Non-exclusive remedies, equitable relief, set-off, third-party rights
