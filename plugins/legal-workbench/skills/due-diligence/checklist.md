# Due diligence checklist

## Corporate
- Incorporation documents, MoA/AoA (bylaws), certificate of incorporation, name changes
- Registers: members, directors, charges, beneficial ownership (India: SBO under s.90 Companies Act)
- Cap table reconciliation with share issuance records, valuation reports, FEMA/FDI filings
  (India: FC-GPR/FC-TRS, pricing guidelines [VERIFY]); ESOP scheme and grants
- Board and shareholder minutes and resolutions for key actions; statutory filings up to date (MCA/Companies House)
- Shareholders' agreements, investor rights, ROFR/tag/drag, anti-dilution, reserved matters
- Subsidiaries, JVs, group structure chart

## Commercial contracts
- Top customers and suppliers by value; change-of-control and assignment clauses
- Exclusivity, non-compete, MFN, minimum purchase obligations
- Related-party transactions and arm's-length basis
- Government contracts (special termination and consent rules)
- Guarantees, comfort letters, indemnities given

## Finance
- Loan agreements, security documents, charges registered; defaults, covenants, change-of-control
  prepayment triggers; consents from lenders needed
- Off-balance-sheet liabilities, contingent liabilities

## Employment
- Key employee contracts, non-solicit/IP assignment, severance terms
- Statutory compliance (India: PF, ESI, gratuity, bonus, Shops and Establishments,
  POSH Internal Committee and annual reports; Labour Codes status [VERIFY])
- Contractors and misclassification risk; disputes, union matters

## IP and technology
- Registered IP (trademarks, patents, designs, copyrights) - owner of record, status, renewals
- Assignments from founders, employees and contractors
- Licences in and out; open-source usage and copyleft exposure
- Domain names, source code escrow, key software

## Data protection and cyber
- Privacy policies, consent mechanisms, data maps, processor agreements
- DPDP Act 2023 / GDPR / sectoral rules (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, CERT-In 6-hour incident reporting [VERIFY])
- Breaches and regulator correspondence

## Real estate
- Title documents, encumbrance certificates, leases/leave and licence, stamp duty and registration
- Land use and building approvals, occupancy certificates

## Regulatory and licences
- Business licences, sector approvals, environmental consents, renewals and conditions
- Anti-bribery, sanctions and export controls exposure; past investigations

## Litigation
- Pending and threatened litigation, arbitrations, notices, regulatory proceedings
  (check court/tribunal portals: eCourts, NCLT, NCDRC; PACER/state; CE-File)
- Consumer complaints, product liability, insolvency petitions against the target

## Tax (usually with tax advisers)
- Direct and indirect tax assessments, demands, disputes; GST registrations; transfer pricing
