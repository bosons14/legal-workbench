# Clause order by document type

**Mutual / one-way NDA:** Parties; Purpose; Definition of Confidential Information;
Exclusions; Obligations (use, disclosure, standard of care); Permitted disclosures
(representatives, legal compulsion with notice); Return/destruction; No licence /
no warranty / no obligation to proceed; Term and survival; Remedies (injunction);
Data protection (if personal data shared); General; Governing law and jurisdiction.

**Master Services Agreement:** Definitions; Structure (MSA + SOWs, precedence);
Services and SOW process; Change control; Customer obligations; Personnel and
subcontracting; Service levels; Acceptance; Fees, invoicing, taxes; IP; Confidentiality;
Data protection and security; Warranties; Indemnities; Limitation of liability;
Insurance; Term and termination; Exit and transition; Force majeure; Non-solicit;
Compliance (anti-bribery, sanctions); Dispute resolution; General.

**SaaS / subscription:** add Access and use restrictions; Acceptable use; Uptime and
support SLA; Customer data; Suspension; Third-party/open-source; AI features and
data-training terms; Subscription term, renewals, price increases.

**Employment (India focus):** Appointment and designation; Commencement and probation;
Duties and place of work (transfer clause); Hours; Remuneration (CTC breakup, statutory
deductions: PF, ESI, gratuity, professional tax); Leave (as per state Shops and
Establishments Act / company policy); Confidentiality; IP assignment; Conflicts and
moonlighting; Non-solicit (non-compete post-employment largely unenforceable - s.27 Indian Contract
Act [VERIFY]); Termination and notice; Garden leave; Return of property; Code of conduct
and POSH policy reference; Data processing notice; Governing law.

**Consultancy / independent contractor:** emphasise independent status, no employment
benefits, own taxes (GST registration if applicable), IP assignment with moral-rights
waiver, deliverable-based fees, termination for convenience, no exclusivity.

**Shareholders' Agreement:** Definitions; Share capital and shareholding; Board
composition and nomination rights; Reserved matters (affirmative votes); Shareholder
meetings and quorum; Information rights; Pre-emptive rights; Transfer restrictions
(lock-in, ROFR/ROFO, tag-along, drag-along); Anti-dilution; Exit (IPO, strategic sale,
put/call); Deadlock; Non-compete/non-solicit of founders; Representations; Events of
default; Confidentiality; Termination; Dispute resolution (often arbitration);
Articles to be amended to reflect SHA (India: enforceability against the company
depends on incorporation into the AoA [VERIFY]).

**Leave and licence / lease (India):** Parties; Premises description; Licence (not
tenancy - no interest in property); Term and lock-in; Licence fee and escalation;
Security deposit (interest-free, refund timeline); Maintenance and society charges;
Utilities; Use; Alterations; Repairs; Access/inspection; Termination and notice;
Handover; Stamp duty and registration (mandatory registration of leave-and-licence
in Maharashtra - Maharashtra Rent Control Act s.55 [VERIFY]); Police verification.

**Amendment / addendum:** identify original agreement (date, parties, any earlier
amendments); definitions incorporated; precise amendments ("Clause 7.2 is deleted and
replaced with: ..."); effective date; all other terms continue; counterparts.

**Settlement agreement:** Background (without admission); Settlement sum and payment
mechanics; Release (scope - known/unknown claims, carve-outs); Withdrawal of
proceedings (consent terms / compromise under O. XXIII CPC in India); Confidentiality and
non-disparagement; No admission; Tax; Breach consequences (revival of claims);
Governing law.
