## Confidentiality - core obligation (B)
Each Receiving Party shall (a) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only
for the Purpose; (b) not disclose it to any person except its Representatives who need to
know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective
than this Clause; and (c) protect it using at least the degree of care it uses to protect
its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than
reasonable care. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Clause by its
Representatives.

## Compelled disclosure (B)
If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or order of a court or competent
authority to disclose Confidential Information, it may do so to the extent required,
provided that it (where lawful) gives the Disclosing Party prompt written notice,
cooperates (at the Disclosing Party's cost) with any attempt to obtain protective
treatment, and discloses only the portion legally required.

## IP indemnity (C)
The Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the Customer and its Affiliates
against all losses, damages, costs (including reasonable legal fees) and liabilities
arising from any claim that the Deliverables or the Customer's use of them in accordance
with this Agreement infringe any third party's intellectual property rights, provided
the Customer (a) notifies the Supplier promptly in writing of the claim; (b) allows the
Supplier sole control of the defence and settlement (except that no settlement admitting
fault or imposing obligations on the Customer may be made without its consent); and
(c) gives reasonable assistance at the Supplier's cost.

## Termination for material breach (B)
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the
other party commits a material breach and (if the breach is remediable) fails to remedy it
within [thirty (30)] days after receiving written notice specifying the breach and requiring
it to be remedied.

## Force majeure (B)
Neither party is in breach of this Agreement or liable for delay or failure in performing
its obligations (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by a Force Majeure
Event, provided it (a) notifies the other party promptly of the event and its likely
duration; and (b) uses reasonable efforts to mitigate its effect. If a Force Majeure Event
prevents performance for more than [sixty (60)] consecutive days, either party may
terminate this Agreement on [fourteen (14)] days' written notice.

## Data protection - processor (C)
The Supplier shall process Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions
and only for the Purpose; implement appropriate technical and organisational security
measures; ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality; not engage sub-processors
without the Customer's prior written authorisation; notify the Customer without undue
delay and in any event within [24/48] hours of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach;
assist the Customer with data principal / data subject requests and breach reporting
obligations under Applicable Data Protection Law; and on termination delete or return all
Personal Data at the Customer's election. ("Applicable Data Protection Law" includes, as
relevant, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules, the GDPR/UK GDPR, and
[US state laws].)

## Notices (B)
Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or registered post with
acknowledgement due, or by email to the addresses in Clause [X] (or as updated by notice).
A notice is deemed received: by hand, on delivery; by courier/registered post, [two (2)]
Business Days after dispatch; by email, at the time of transmission if sent before 5:00 pm
(recipient's local time) on a Business Day, otherwise on the next Business Day, provided no
bounce-back is received.

## Entire agreement (B)
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties relating to its
subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and representations.
Each party acknowledges that it has not relied on any statement, representation or
warranty not set out in this Agreement. Nothing in this Clause limits liability for fraud.
