# Litigation skeletons (adapt to the forum's current rules and practice directions)

## India - Civil suit (plaint, O. VII CPC)
```
IN THE COURT OF [ ] AT [ ]
CIVIL SUIT NO. ___ OF 20__
IN THE MATTER OF:
[Plaintiff - name, s/o|d/o|w/o, age, occupation, address]   ...PLAINTIFF
VERSUS
[Defendant - details]                                          ...DEFENDANT
SUIT FOR [RECOVERY OF RS. ___ / PERMANENT INJUNCTION / SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE ...]
MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:
1. [Parties]
2-n. [Facts, chronologically, each para one fact cluster, documents annexed]
n+1. Cause of action arose on [dates] when ...
n+2. Jurisdiction - territorial / pecuniary
n+3. Limitation - the suit is within limitation under Article [ ] as ...
n+4. Valuation and court fee
n+5. [Commercial suits: pre-institution mediation under s.12A Commercial Courts Act complied /
     urgent interim relief sought - and Statement of Truth per Commercial Courts Act Schedule]
PRAYER - It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) ... (b) ... (c) costs; (d) any other relief deemed fit.
PLAINTIFF through COUNSEL     Place / Date
VERIFICATION - Verified at [ ] on [ ] that paras 1-__ are true to my knowledge, paras __ are
based on legal advice believed to be true ...
[Affidavit in support]
```

## India - Writ petition (Art. 226 / Art. 32)
Index; Synopsis and List of Dates; Memo of Parties; Petition (Facts; Questions of Law;
Grounds A, B, C...; averment that no other similar petition filed; Prayer; Interim
Prayer); Affidavit; Annexures P-1...; Vakalatnama; court fee. Address alternative
remedy and maintainability up front.

## India - Section 138 NI Act demand notice
Timeline (verify current law): notice within **30 days** of receiving the bank's
dishonour memo; drawer has **15 days** from receipt of notice to pay; complaint within
**1 month** after that 15-day period expires (s.142(1)(b)); compute with
`limitation.py in-ni138-notice / in-ni138-payment / in-ni138-complaint`.
```
[Advocate letterhead]              By Regd. Post AD / Speed Post / Email
Date:
To, [Drawer - name and address]
Sub: Legal notice under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
Under instructions from my client [name, address] ("my client"), I state:
1. That you issued cheque no. [ ] dated [ ] for Rs. [ ] drawn on [bank, branch] in discharge of
   [legally enforceable debt/liability - describe].
2. That the cheque was presented on [ ] and returned unpaid on [ ] with the remark
   "[Funds Insufficient / Exceeds Arrangement / ...]" vide memo dated [ ], received by my client on [ ].
3. I hereby call upon you to pay the said amount of Rs. [ ] (cheque amount) to my client within
   fifteen (15) days of receipt of this notice, failing which my client shall initiate proceedings
   under Sections 138 and 142 of the NI Act ... at your risk as to costs and consequences.
[Advocate signature]   Copy retained for record.
```
Demand only the cheque amount in the s.138 demand (other sums - interest, costs - are
claimed separately; demanding a different sum can invalidate the notice [VERIFY]).

## India - Reply to legal notice
Without prejudice heading (if settlement intended); preliminary: notice misconceived;
parawise reply (admit/deny); true facts; counter-claims or demands; denial of
liability; reservation of rights.

## US federal - Complaint
Caption (court, parties, case no.); Introduction; Parties; Jurisdiction and Venue
(28 U.S.C. §§ 1331/1332, 1391); Factual Allegations (numbered); Claims for Relief
(Count I... each with elements); Prayer for Relief; Jury Demand; signature block with
Rule 11 compliance. Plausibility standard: *Twombly* / *Iqbal* [VERIFY cites].

## US federal - Motion to dismiss (12(b)(6))
Notice of motion; Memorandum of law: Introduction, Background (as alleged),
Legal Standard, Argument (I, II, III - headings that state conclusions),
Conclusion. Check local rules on page limits, meet-and-confer, proposed order.

## England & Wales - Particulars of Claim
Heading (court, claim no., parties); numbered paragraphs: parties; contract/duty;
breach (particularised); causation; loss and damage (particularised); interest
(s.35A Senior Courts Act 1981 / s.69 County Courts Act 1984 [VERIFY]); prayer
("AND THE CLAIMANT CLAIMS:"); Statement of Truth in the current CPR PD 22 form [VERIFY wording].
Comply with the relevant pre-action protocol first.

## Written submissions / skeleton arguments
Cover: case details, hearing date, party. Body: (1) Introduction and relief sought;
(2) Short facts with record references; (3) Issues; (4) Submissions issue by issue -
proposition, record reference, authority with pinpoint; (5) Conclusion. Respect page
limits. Attach a list of authorities with copies or links.
